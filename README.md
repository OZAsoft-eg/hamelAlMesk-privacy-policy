# Privacy Policy for Hamel Al-Mesk (حامل المسك)

**Effective date and last updated: September 20, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hamel Al-Mesk (حامل المسك) educational application (the **“App,” “Hamel Al-Mesk,” “we,” “us,”** or **“our”**) collects, uses, stores, and shares information. The App is designed for kindergarten children and is used with accounts provided or authorized by a parent, guardian, school, nursery, or other educational institution.

By allowing a child to use the App, the child’s parent or legal guardian acknowledges the practices described in this Privacy Policy. An educational institution that provides access to the App is responsible for obtaining any authorization or parental consent required by applicable law.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information provided to us or by an educational institution

The App may process the following information:

- **Login information:** username and password submitted to authenticate an existing student account. The App does not offer public, self-service account creation.
- **Student profile information:** student identifier, student name, profile image or avatar, grade, assigned courses, and similar school-account information returned by the educational service.
- **Support communications:** information included when a parent, guardian, or institution contacts us for help, privacy inquiries, or deletion requests.

### Information collected automatically

When the App connects to our services or Firebase, we may collect:

- Internet Protocol (IP) address and basic network information;
- app version, operating system, device model, language, and general device information;
- Firebase installation identifiers and similar app-instance identifiers;
- crash reports, stack traces, error messages, timestamps, and technical diagnostic information; and
- limited request and response metadata needed to diagnose service failures, such as the requested URL, request method, response status, and time of the request.

We do not use this information to advertise to children or to build advertising profiles.

### Information stored on the device

The App stores language preferences, login status, student session/profile data, and an authentication token on the device. Authentication tokens are stored using secure device storage where supported. This information is used to keep the student signed in and personalize educational content.

## 2. Information and Device Features We Do Not Collect

The current version of the App:

- does not display third-party advertising;
- does not sell personal information;
- does not request precise or approximate location;
- does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs, or photos/media library;
- does not process payments or payment-card information; and
- does not provide chat, public posting, or social-networking features.

Interactive drawing, tracing, coloring, matching, and similar exercise input is used to operate the activity on the device and is not intended to be uploaded as personal content.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- authenticate students and maintain their sessions;
- provide the correct grade, courses, lessons, books, media, and exercises;
- display the student’s name and avatar within the App;
- deliver and configure App functionality;
- maintain security, prevent unauthorized access, and troubleshoot technical issues;
- diagnose crashes and improve reliability and performance;
- respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not use children’s information for targeted advertising, behavioral advertising, or sale to data brokers.

## 4. How We Share Information

We disclose information only as needed for the purposes described above:

- **Educational service and account provider:** the school, nursery, or educational service that issued or administers the student account may receive and control student account information and educational content assignments.
- **Backend hosting and service systems:** account credentials and requests are transmitted securely to the Hamel Al-Mesk educational backend to authenticate the student and provide content.
- **Google Firebase:** we use Firebase Remote Config to configure the App, Firebase Crashlytics to identify and diagnose crashes, and Cloud Firestore to receive technical error and service diagnostics. Google may process installation identifiers, device and app information, IP addresses, crash data, and diagnostic records as our service provider. More information is available in [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Legal and safety reasons:** we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or valid government requests; enforce our terms; or protect users, the public, or our rights and systems.
- **Business transfer:** information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy and applicable law.
- **With authorization:** we may disclose information when a parent, guardian, institution, or other authorized party directs or consents to the disclosure.

We do not share personal information with third parties for their independent advertising or marketing purposes.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Hamel Al-Mesk is designed for kindergarten children. We therefore seek to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide and protect the educational service.

Student accounts are expected to be created or provided by an authorized parent, guardian, school, nursery, or educational institution. The App does not allow children to publicly post information, communicate with unknown users, make purchases, or receive behavioral advertising.

Parents and legal guardians may ask to review, correct, or delete their child’s personal information, or withdraw consent where applicable, by using the contact information below. We may need to verify the requester’s identity and authority. If an educational institution controls the student account, we may direct the request to, or coordinate with, that institution.

If you believe that a child’s information was provided without appropriate authorization or consent, please contact us promptly.

## 6. Legal Bases and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, our legal bases for processing may include providing the educational service requested by a parent, guardian, or institution; consent; compliance with legal obligations; and legitimate interests such as securing, maintaining, and improving the App, provided those interests do not override the rights of the child.

Subject to applicable law, a parent, guardian, or authorized user may have the right to:

- request access to or a copy of personal information;
- correct inaccurate information;
- request deletion of personal information;
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- request data portability; and
- complain to a competent data-protection authority.

We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising applicable privacy rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain the student account, meet educational and contractual requirements, resolve disputes, protect security, and comply with law.

- Local authenticated session data is kept on the device until the user logs out, clears App data, or uninstalls the App.
- Student account records held by the educational service are retained according to the institution’s requirements and applicable law.
- Crash and diagnostic records are retained for a limited period appropriate for troubleshooting, security, and service improvement, after which they are deleted or de-identified unless a longer period is legally required.
- Support and deletion-request records may be retained as needed to document and complete the request and meet legal obligations.

Backup copies may remain for a limited period before being overwritten or deleted through normal backup cycles.

## 8. Deletion Requests

Logging out removes the locally stored authenticated session and authentication token, but it does not delete the student account from the educational service. Uninstalling the App removes App data from the device but does not by itself delete server-side account records.

To request deletion of a student account or associated personal information, a parent, guardian, or authorized institution may email **info@hamelalmesk.com** with:

- the subject line **“Hamel Al-Mesk Data Deletion Request”**;
- the student username or identifier (do not send the password); and
- the requester’s relationship to the student and the institution that issued the account, if applicable.

We will verify the requester’s identity and authority and will delete or de-identify information that we control, unless retention is required or permitted by law. Where an educational institution controls the account, the institution may need to approve or complete the request.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. These include encrypted network transport, access controls, and secure device storage for authentication tokens where supported. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Please do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Our service providers, including Google Firebase, may process information in countries other than the country where the user lives. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers. Firebase states that its services may process data on global infrastructure; details are available on its privacy page linked above.

## 11. Third-Party Links and Services

The App may load educational content from services operated for Hamel Al-Mesk. If the App links to an independent third-party site or service, that third party’s privacy practices apply, and we are not responsible for those practices.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable requirements. We will post the revised policy and update the date at the top. When required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
